Google Chrome is open on the Wrenfield Makerspace parts-requests page, and LibreOffice Calc is open on the purchase log, `purchase-log.fods`.

Bring the purchase log up to date:

- For every request that is Approved and not yet logged, add one row to the sheet "Purchases 2026", directly below the rows already there.
- Fill the four columns from the request: the request number in Request, the part exactly as written on the request page in Part, the approved quantity in Qty, and the member who asked for it in Requested by.
- Add the new rows in order of request number, lowest first.
- Save the spreadsheet, keeping its current format.
- Then mark exactly those requests as logged on the request page.

Keep the rows already in the sheet as they are. Do not mark any other request as logged.

Use only Chrome and LibreOffice Calc. Do not close either app.
