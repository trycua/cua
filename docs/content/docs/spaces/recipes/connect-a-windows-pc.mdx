Use a Windows PC from your Mac.

The Cua Spaces app runs on a Mac. A Windows PC joins from the other direction:
you run one install command on the PC, and the PC then shows up in Cua Spaces
on your Mac.

## You need

- A Windows PC that is switched on and signed in to Windows.
- Cua Spaces on your Mac, signed in to your Cua account.
- A few minutes at the PC, once. You will paste one line into PowerShell.

## Steps

Do steps 1 to 4 on the Windows PC.

1. Open the Start menu, type **PowerShell** and open it.
2. Paste this line and press Enter:

   ```powershell skip="host-install"
   irm https://cua.ai/install.ps1 | iex
   ```

3. When the list of items appears, type `3` and press Enter, so that **Host this machine** shows `[x]`. Press Enter again to install.
4. When your browser opens, sign in with the same Cua account you use on your Mac. Then go back to PowerShell and wait for it to finish.
5. On your Mac, open Cua Spaces, click the PC in the sidebar and click **Open**.

## It worked if

- The PC appears in the sidebar of Cua Spaces on your Mac and says it is online.
- After you click **Open**, you see the PC's desktop and can use it.

## If it didn't

- **The PC isn't in the list.** Check that it is switched on, awake and signed in to Windows, and that you used the same Cua account on both. It can take a moment to appear.
- **PowerShell says it can't finish setting up.** Run `cua host setup` in the same window. Run `cua auth login` first if it asks you to sign in.
- **Your Mac says it needs enrolling.** Open **Settings > Devices** and click **Enroll**. Choose **Sign in again**.
