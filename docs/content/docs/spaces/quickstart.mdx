this computer your first Space and ask your agent to use it while you watch.

## Before you start

- A Mac (macOS 26 or later), a Windows PC (preview) or a Linux PC (Ubuntu 24.04).
- An AI coding agent, such as Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. See the
  [supported agents](/cua-cli/guides/mcp-server#supported-agents).
- A free Cua account. The app asks you to sign in during setup.

Your computer is your first Space, so there is nothing big to download. An extra
Space is optional: a Linux Space needs a container engine such as Docker, and a
macOS Space needs an Apple silicon Mac and about 50 GB of free disk.

## 1. Install Cua Spaces

<Tabs items={['macOS and Linux', 'Windows (preview)']}>
  <Tab value="macOS and Linux">
    ```bash skip="host-install"
    curl -fsSL https://cua.ai/install.sh | sh
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Windows (preview)">
    ```powershell skip="host-install"
    irm https://cua.ai/install.ps1 | iex
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The command installs the `cua` command and the app. You can also download the
app from the [download page](https://cua.ai/download). [Install](/spaces/guides/install)
has the details for each system.

## 2. Connect your agent

Open Cua Spaces. The first screen says "A shared Space for every agent."

Press **Connect Claude Code**. If you use another agent, pick it instead:
Codex, Hermes, OpenClaw or **Other**. The app adds Cua to that agent's settings.

If your agent was already open, close it and open it again so it loads the new
tools. To connect more agents later, see [Connect your agent](/spaces/guides/connect-your-agent).

## 3. Make this computer your first Space

The app checks your setup and shows what's left. These steps use the Mac
wording; on Windows and Linux the app says "this PC" or "this computer". Press the button on each row:

1. **Sign in**, then finish signing in to Cua in your browser. The app then
   adds this Mac to your account by itself.
2. **Let Cua control this Mac**, so your agent can click and type.
3. **Let Cua see this screen**, so your agent can see what's on it.

Each of the last two opens System Settings. Turn Cua on there; macOS asks you
only once. When every row is done, the app says "Your agent can use this Mac."
This Mac is now your first Space.

## 4. Try your first request

Under **Try this in Claude Code**, press **Copy** and paste the request into
your agent:

> List my computers and tell me what's on this Mac

It works right away, on this Mac. Open Cua Spaces to watch your agent work, and
use the desktop yourself whenever you want to take over.

## 5. Add a Linux Space (optional)

**Give your agent its own Space** offers a fresh computer inside this Mac, away
from your files and your mouse. Choose **Linux** for the quickest one (a Mac can
also make a macOS Space), or choose **No extra Space for now**. The card shows
how long it takes and how much it downloads.

Press **Done** whenever you like. The new Space keeps getting made after the
window closes, shows its progress in the sidebar, and you get a notice when it's
ready.

<Callout type="info">
  If a step is still open, the button says **Finish later**. Home then keeps a
  **Finish setting up** list with the same steps. A Space that fails to create
  shows the reason and a **Try again** button. See
  [Troubleshoot](/spaces/guides/troubleshoot).
</Callout>
