BenchLab is already open on your Mac. Its main window shows a greeting panel and a "Confirm ticket" field.

1. Open BenchLab's Settings window from the menu bar (the BenchLab menu, then Settings...).
2. In Settings, change these three settings, then press Apply:
   - Display name: {{name}}
   - Theme: {{theme}}
   - Compact layout: turn it {{compact}}
3. Go back to the main BenchLab window. After Apply, the panel shows a ticket number. Type that number into the "Confirm ticket" field and press Confirm.

The task is complete when the status line in the main window reads "Confirmed". Do not close BenchLab.
