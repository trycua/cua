Teams turns the computers your team already has into shared desktops for
people and their agents. With Teams, machines belong to the team instead of one
person's account, an owner and admins decide who can do what, and a new device
must be approved before it can reach a team machine. You bring the computers;
Cua does not rent them ([FAQ](/spaces/guides/teams/faq)).

| | |
|---|---|
| Price | $30 per seat a month, or $300 per seat a year (two months free) |
| Seats | 1 to 100 |
| Includes | Everything in Pro, plus team machines, roles and device approval |
| Usage | Each seat adds 5 live sessions and 200 relay hours a month to one pool the whole team shares |

You can start with 1 seat, just you, and add seats as people join. Two seats
give 10 live sessions and 400 relay hours a month for the team.

A **live session** is one person or agent holding a stream open to a machine
through the relay, such as a desktop view, a terminal or an agent's process
stream. A **relay hour** is an hour with at least one live session, counted per
calendar month (UTC). Spaces on your own network or reached directly do not go
through the relay and do not count. If the team goes over, nothing is cut off:
the Cua Spaces app shows a notice with an Upgrade button.

Prices and usage are on [cua.ai/pricing](https://cua.ai/pricing).

## Buy Teams

1. Open [app.cua.ai/buy/teams](https://app.cua.ai/buy/teams) and sign in or
   create a Cua account.
2. Name your team and choose the number of seats and monthly or yearly billing.
3. Pay with Stripe Checkout. Teams is for business use, so checkout asks for
   your business name and tax ID.

There is no trial. The person who buys is the team's owner.

## Create your team and invite people

Naming the team in checkout creates it, and you join it as its **owner**. After
payment the Team page at [app.cua.ai/team](https://app.cua.ai/team) shows
"Invite your team".

Teams are invitation only. Enter an email address and choose a role. The person
gets an email, signs in with that address and joins. Invitees do not pay and do
not see checkout.

- Members plus pending invitations cannot exceed the seats you pay for, and
  you take one seat yourself. On a 1-seat team, the Team page shows **Add
  seats** before your first invite; it opens Stripe, where you raise the
  quantity (see [Seats and billing](#seats-and-billing)).
- Until the first payment completes, a new team can invite up to 5 people.
- Revoke a pending invitation from the same page.

## Roles

A team has one owner, and any number of admins and members.

| | Owner | Admin | Member |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open team machines (from an approved device) | yes | yes | yes |
| Request approval for a new device | not needed | yes | yes |
| Leave the team | no | yes | yes |
| Invite people | yes | yes | no |
| Remove members | yes, admins too | members only | no |
| Add and remove team machines | yes | yes | no |
| Approve or deny devices | yes | yes | no |
| Make a member an admin, or an admin a member | yes | no | no |
| Cancel the subscription, delete the team | yes | no | no |

The owner's role cannot change and the owner cannot leave: to close a team,
cancel the subscription first, then delete the team. The owner's own devices
need no approval. Admins cannot remove other admins or the owner.

## Remove a member or leave

Owners and admins remove a member in the Members list on the Team page.
Removal takes effect at once, and the person's device approvals for the team are
deleted. A member leaves with **Leave team** on the same page.

## Seats and billing

Seats, the monthly or yearly period, the card and invoices are managed in the
Stripe billing portal: [app.cua.ai/billing](https://app.cua.ai/billing), then
**Manage billing**. Only the owner buys and changes the plan.

- **Add seats:** you pay a prorated amount for the rest of the current period,
  added to your next invoice.
- **Remove seats:** you get a prorated credit on future invoices. Credits are
  not refunded in cash. You cannot go below 1 seat.
- **Switch between monthly and yearly:** in the same portal. Stripe prorates the
  change.

See the [Plan Terms](https://cua.ai/spaces-terms) for the details.

## Cancel

Cancel in the billing portal at any time. The plan stays active until the end of
the period you paid for, then the team moves to Free. Monthly plans have no
refund for partial months. On a yearly plan, cancelling within 14 days of the
first yearly charge refunds it in full. Your Spaces and everything in them stay
on your machines.
