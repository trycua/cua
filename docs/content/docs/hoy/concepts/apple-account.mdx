---
title: Apple Account and iCloud
description: 'What works and what does not when you use Apple services inside a Hoy macOS VM.'
---

You can't sign in to an Apple Account in System Settings inside a Hoy VM.
Apple only allows that sign-in in VMs that run on Apple's own Virtualization
framework. Hoy runs VMs on its own engine, so the system-wide Apple Account
sign-in is not available, and features that depend on it don't work.

## What works

- **iCloud on the web.** Open [icloud.com](https://www.icloud.com) in Safari
  or another browser in the VM, and use "Sign in with Apple" on websites.
- **iCloud Mail, Calendar and Contacts.** Create an
  [app-specific password](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102654) for your
  Apple Account, then add the account in Mail, Calendar or Contacts with that
  password.
- **CloudKit web services.** Apps that use CloudKit's web services work as
  they do on any Mac.
- **Xcode code signing.** Sign with an App Store Connect API key or with
  certificates and provisioning profiles you install in the VM. Download Xcode
  from [developer.apple.com](https://developer.apple.com/download/applications/)
  rather than the App Store.

## What doesn't work

- Signing in to an Apple Account in System Settings.
- iCloud Drive sync, iCloud Keychain and iCloud Photos.
- Messages (iMessage) and FaceTime.
- The App Store, including downloading apps you've bought.
- Xcode's Accounts sign-in. Use an App Store Connect API key instead.

This is a known limitation. If you need it for your work, tell us at
[hoy@cua.ai](mailto:hoy@cua.ai).
