## Is SSO included?

Not yet. SSO and audit export are coming next, with no dates yet. They are not
included in Teams today.

## Does Cua host or rent the machines?

No. Spaces run on computers you own or rent yourself: your Mac, other machines
you connect, or VMs in your own cloud account. Your cloud provider bills you for
those VMs. Cua provides the app, the sharing and the relay that lets your
devices reach your machines.

## Can a Mac be a team machine?

Yes. Macs, Linux and Windows machines can all be team machines, so you can share
your own Mac with your team. See [team machines](/spaces/guides/teams/machines).

## What happens to our data if we cancel?

Your Spaces and everything in them stay on your machines. The team moves to Free
at the end of the period you paid for.

## Is there a trial?

No. Free stays available, and you can [buy Teams](/spaces/guides/teams#buy-teams) when
you are ready.

## What is the refund policy?

Monthly plans have no refund for partial months. On a yearly plan, cancel within
14 days of the first yearly charge for a full refund; after that there is no
refund for the unused part of the year. The
[Plan Terms](https://cua.ai/spaces-terms) have the full text.

## How are seat changes charged?

Stripe prorates them. Added seats are charged pro rata for the rest of the
period on your next invoice. Removed seats earn a prorated credit on future
invoices, not a cash refund. The minimum is 1 seat, the maximum 100.

## What if we use more than the pool?

Nothing is cut off. The Cua Spaces app shows a notice with an Upgrade button.
See [Teams](/spaces/guides/teams) for what a live session and a relay hour are.

## Which Spaces count against the pool?

Only those that use the relay. Spaces on your own network or reached directly do not go through the relay and
do not count.
