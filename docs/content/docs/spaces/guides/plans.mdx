Cua Spaces is bring-your-own compute: you connect your own machines, and Cua
does not rent them out. The plans cover the app, sharing and the hosted relay
that lets your devices reach your machines. Current prices are on
[cua.ai/pricing](https://cua.ai/pricing), which is the source of truth.

| Plan | Price | Live sessions | Relay hours |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 2 | about 20 a month |
| Pro | $18 a month or $180 a year, one person | 5 | 200 a month |
| Teams | $30 per seat a month or $300 per seat a year, 1 to 100 seats | 5 per seat | 200 per seat a month, pooled across the team |

A **live session** is one person viewing or driving a Space through the relay,
such as a desktop view, a terminal or an agent's process stream. A **relay
hour** is an hour with at least one live session, counted per calendar month
(UTC). Spaces you reach directly, for example on your own network, do not use
the relay.

Teams starts at 1 seat. Each seat adds to the team pool, so 2 seats give 10
live sessions and 400 relay hours a month. To set up a team, see [Teams](/spaces/guides/teams).
