## share_space

Let another cua.ai account watch or edit a Space.

Shares the Space through the cua.ai relay with one account, by verified email or account id; the same allowlist and device enrollment `cua host share` uses. A viewer joins presence with their own named cursor and watches a view-only stream: the Space's driver refuses their input, shell, files and MCP. An editor gets full use, and their input is a human session. Sharing again changes the role. A host (`relay:<id>`) is shared directly; any other Space (local, cloud, or added by address) is attached to the relay on its first share: its own driver dials out, so it needs no inbound port. Invitees open `invitee_space`.

## unshare_space

Stop sharing a Space with an account, or with everyone.

Removes one account (or everyone) at once: the relay cuts their open streams and refuses new calls. With no `who`, the Space stops being shared and a Space that is not a host leaves the relay.

## relay_register_space

Publish a Space on the relay for the account's other devices.

Publishes the Space as a machine of the signed-in account on the cua.ai relay, the way `cua host setup` publishes this computer: the Space's own driver joins the relay over outbound WSS with a machine token the account registered, so no inbound port is opened. The account's other devices (a phone off this network, the Cua app on another computer) then open `relay_space`; nobody else can until share_space adds them. Idempotent, and it re-attaches a Space that restarted. Needs a driver with relay_attach (a host is already registered).
