A Space on the relay carries `SpaceInfo.sharing`: its owner, your role (`owner`, `editor`, `viewer`), the team you reach it through and, for your own, who else has it. `leave_many` takes you off Spaces shared with you; `delete_many` deletes several of yours, three at a time, turning running ones off first. Both answer one `BulkResult` per id, in order.

## Leave and bulk delete

Methods of [`Spaces`](/cua-sdk/reference/spaces#spaces).

### `Spaces.leave_many`

Takes this account off Spaces shared with it (through the relay): a
direct share ends, a team's Space leaves this account's list. Your
own Spaces are refused (delete them). One result per id, in order;
a relay that predates leaving answers "Couldn't leave &lt;name&gt;: the
Cua relay needs an update."

## On the relay

Methods of [`Spaces`](/cua-sdk/reference/spaces#spaces).

### `Spaces.relay_register`

Publishes `space` on the cua.ai relay as a machine of the signed-in
account, the way `cua host setup` publishes this computer: the
Space's own driver dials out, so the account's other devices (a
phone off this network) reach it as `relay:<machine>`, and nobody
else until it is shared. Idempotent.

## `SpaceSharing` record

Who a Space belongs to and who else has it, as this account sees it
(`SpaceInfo.sharing`).

| Field | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `owner_id` / `ownerId` | `String` |  | The owner's account id. |
| `owner_email` / `ownerEmail` | `String` | `""` | The owner's email, when known. |
| `owner_name` / `ownerName` | `String` | `""` | The owner's display name, when known. |
| `role` | `String` |  | What this account may do: `owner`, `editor` or `viewer`. |
| `team` | `String` | `""` | The team this account reaches it through (its key), when it is a team's and not shared with this account directly. |
| `team_name` / `teamName` | `String` | `""` | That team's display name (its key when the name is not known). |
| `editors` | `Vec<String>` | `[]` | Owner view: accounts (emails or ids) that may use it. |
| `viewers` | `Vec<String>` | `[]` | Owner view: accounts that may only watch. |
| `teams` | [`Vec<SpaceTeamShare>`](#spaceteamshare-record) | `[]` | Owner view: the teams it is a machine of. |

## `BulkResult` record

| Field | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `ok` | `bool` |  | It went through. |
| `error` | `Option<String>` |  | Why not, in plain words. |
| `message` | `String` |  | What happened, for people. |
