## Bug reports

Open an issue at [github.com/trycua/cua/issues](https://github.com/trycua/cua/issues).
Search first, in case someone has already reported it.

Include:

- the output of `hoy --version`;
- the output of `hoy doctor`;
- what you ran, what you expected and what happened, with the error text;
- for a VM problem, the last lines of `hoy logs <vm>`.

Before you write, [Troubleshooting](/hoy/guides/troubleshooting) covers the errors
people hit most.

## Security reports

Do not open a public issue for a security problem. Write to
[support@trycua.com](mailto:support@trycua.com?subject=Hoy%20security) with
"Hoy security" in the subject.

## Questions

For anything else, such as licensing or your setup, write to
[hoy@cua.ai](mailto:hoy@cua.ai).
