---
title: Connect your other Mac
description: 'Set up a second Mac once, then see and use its desktop from this Mac, wherever each one is.'
---

Use your other Mac from this one, from anywhere.

## You need

- Cua Spaces on both Macs (macOS 26 or later), signed in to the same Cua account.
- The other Mac switched on, awake and logged in.
- A few minutes at the other Mac, once.

## Steps

Do steps 1 to 5 on the other Mac.

1. Go to [spaces.cua.ai](https://spaces.cua.ai) and click **Download for Mac**. Open the file and drag the Cua Spaces icon onto Applications.
2. Open Cua Spaces and click through the welcome pages. When you reach **Sign in**, sign in with the same account you use on your first Mac.
3. On **How will you use this machine?**, choose **Set up this machine for unattended access**.
4. Leave **Use for** on **Its desktop** and click **Set up for access**.
5. Under **Grant in System Settings**, click **Open Settings** and switch on each permission it lists.
6. Back on your first Mac, open Cua Spaces, click the other Mac in the sidebar and click **Open**.

## It worked if

- The other Mac appears in the sidebar of your first Mac and says it is online.
- After you click **Open**, you see the other Mac's desktop, and it follows your pointer and keys.
- On the other Mac, **This machine** says **Sharing**.

## If it didn't

- **The other Mac isn't in the list.** Check that both Macs show the same account email in Settings. Make sure the other Mac is on, awake and logged in, then wait a moment and look again.
- **This Mac says it needs enrolling.** Open **Settings > Devices** and click **Enroll**. Choose **Sign in again**, or choose **Approve from another device** and approve the code on a Mac that is already enrolled. If **Devices** lists the other Mac under **New Machines**, click **Confirm**.
- **You see the desktop but can't click, or the screen is blank.** On the other Mac, open **This machine** and grant every permission under **Grant in System Settings**.
