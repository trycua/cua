LibreOffice Calc is open on dues.fods, the Carrowdale Rowing Club's dues ledger for the 2026-27 season (sheet "Dues 2026-27").

Tidy up the formatting of the sheet:

- Freeze the header row (row 1) so that it stays visible while scrolling down. Do not freeze any columns.
- Make the header row (Member, Category, Due date, Amount, Paid on, Notes) bold.
- Format the Amount column as currency in euros with two decimal places, so that 72.5 shows with the € sign and as 72.50.
- Set the Notes column to wrap text inside its cells.

Do not change any value, do not make the member rows bold, and do not add, remove or reorder rows or columns. Save the document in its current format (keep the name dues.fods).

Use only LibreOffice Calc. Do not close LibreOffice.
