Cua Spaces gives your AI agents their own computers to work on, and lets you
watch, step in and share them. You bring the computers: a Mac on your desk, a
spare Linux box, a VM in your own cloud account. Cua does not rent them.

A **Space** is one desktop on one of those computers, set up for a task. Your
agent runs commands in it and uses its apps and browser. You see the same
screen, take over when you want, and give a teammate access.

## Get started

1. [Quickstart](/spaces/quickstart): install the app, connect your agent and
   run a first task. About five minutes.
2. [Connect your agent](/spaces/guides/connect-your-agent): add Spaces to
   Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other agents.
3. [Add a machine](/spaces/guides/add-a-machine): connect another Mac, Linux
   or Windows computer you own.

## For developers

The `cua` CLI, the MCP server and the SDKs (Python, TypeScript, Swift and
Rust) use the same Spaces as the app.

- [Use Spaces from an agent](/spaces/guides/use-from-an-agent): the Spaces
  tools your agent gets over MCP.
- [Create a Space with the Cua SDK](/spaces/guides/create-a-space-with-the-sdk):
  create, use and delete a Space from code.
- [Reference](/spaces/reference): every Spaces tool, its parameters and its
  errors.
- [Examples](/spaces/examples/cua-bots): apps built on the Spaces SDK.
