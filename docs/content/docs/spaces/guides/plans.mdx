Cua Spaces is bring-your-own compute: you connect your own machines, and Cua
does not rent them out. The plans cover the app, sharing and the hosted relay
that lets your devices reach your machines. Current prices are on
[cua.ai/pricing](https://cua.ai/pricing), which is the source of truth.

| Plan | Price | Live sessions | Remote hours |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 2 | 20 a month |
| Pro | $18 a month or $180 a year, one person | 5 | 200 a month |
| Teams | $30 per seat a month or $300 per seat a year, 1 to 100 seats | 5 per seat | 200 per seat a month, pooled across the team |

Every plan includes Spaces on every computer you connect, Teleport and sharing a
Space with named people. Pro adds more live sessions and remote hours. Teams adds
team-owned machines, roles and device approval. SSO and audit export are not
included yet.

## What counts toward the limits

**Remote hours** are the time you spend using your computers from somewhere
else. Using a computer from itself is free.

In detail, a **live session** is one person or agent holding a stream open to a
machine through the relay, such as a desktop view, a terminal or an agent's
process stream. Each hour with at least one live session counts as one remote
hour, per calendar month (UTC).

Spaces you reach directly, for example on your own network, don't use the relay
and don't count.

## See your usage

Open **Settings** in Cua Spaces. The usage row shows your live sessions and
remote hours this month, for example 1 of 2 live sessions and 6.5 of 20 remote
hours, and the plan card shows your plan. To read the same numbers in a
terminal, run `cua usage`.

When you have used 80% of your remote hours, the app shows a notice with an
**Upgrade** button. Nothing stops at the limit: your Spaces keep working.

## Upgrade to Pro

1. Press **Upgrade** or **Get Pro** in the app, or open
   [cua.ai/pricing](https://cua.ai/pricing) and choose **Get Pro**.
2. Sign in or create a Cua account.
3. Pay with Stripe Checkout.

Pro has no trial, and Free stays available. On a yearly plan, you can cancel
within 14 days of the first yearly charge for a full refund. Monthly plans have
no refund for partial months. Change or cancel a plan at any time on the
[billing page](https://app.cua.ai/billing).

## Start a team

Teams starts at 1 seat, so you can begin with just yourself. Each seat adds to
one pool the whole team shares: 2 seats give 10 live sessions and 400 remote hours
a month. To buy Teams, invite people and manage seats, see
[Teams](/spaces/guides/teams).
