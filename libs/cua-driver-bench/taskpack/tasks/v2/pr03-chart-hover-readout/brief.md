Google Chrome is open on the Sallowmead Depot Returns page, which charts the parcels that came back to the depot each week from week 30 to week 41, in two series: "Returned by customers" and "Refused at the door". The chart shows a bar's exact figure only while the pointer rests on that bar.

Find the week in which the most parcels were returned by customers (the "Returned by customers" series only) and that week's exact figure.

In the weekly review answer below the chart, choose that week under "Week with the most returns", enter the exact figure under "Number of returns that week", and submit the answer once. Leave the depot set to Sallowmead North.

Use only the web page in Chrome. Do not close Chrome.
