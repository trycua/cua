# Provenance

The screenshots, bitmap glyphs, annotations, generator, and documentation in
this directory were created for Cua. They are synthetic and do not derive from
third-party screenshots, product artwork, fonts, icons, model output, customer
data, or recorded desktop sessions.

`generate.py` draws RGB pixels from geometric primitives, an original compact
5x7 bitmap alphabet embedded in the script, and fixed-seed pseudo-random noise.
The browser and remote-desktop scenarios are fictional interfaces. Names and
values are generic test strings and do not identify real users or systems.

The generated PNGs and `manifest.json` are checked in so tests and reviews do
not require a rendering dependency. `python3 generate.py --check` regenerates
the corpus in a temporary directory and compares every generated byte with the
checked-in files.
