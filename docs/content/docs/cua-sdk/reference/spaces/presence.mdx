`join_presence` joins the Space's channel. `join_presence_for` takes the window being watched: a window another Space shows here, streamed straight from its origin, joins the origin's channel and this Space's and reads them as one. The types are on [Streams and presence](/cua-sdk/reference/spaces/streams).

## Joining presence

Methods of [`Space`](/cua-sdk/reference/spaces/space#space).

### `Space.join_presence`

Joins presence. Waits up to `timeout_ms` (default 10 s) for the
roster.

### `Space.join_presence_for`

Joins presence for the window being watched. For a window another
Space shows here and the viewer streams straight from its origin
(`window_id` from `Space.windows`), this joins the origin's channel
and this Space's, publishes the cursor to both and reads them as one:
one avatar and one cursor per viewer, as if the stream came through
this Space. For any other window, or `None`, it is
`Space.join_presence`.
