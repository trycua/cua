LibreOffice Calc is open on orders.fods, the September flour orders of the Brackenford Bakers' Co-operative.

The "Orders" sheet lists 20 orders (date, member, flour code, kilos), with empty "Rate" and "Line cost" columns. The "Rates" sheet gives the price per kilo for each flour code.

Complete the two empty columns for all 20 orders:

- Rate (column E): a formula that looks up the order's flour code on the "Rates" sheet and returns its price per kilo.
- Line cost (column F): a formula that multiplies the order's kilos by its rate.

Every cell in these two columns must be a formula, not a typed number. Do not change the dates, members, flour codes or kilos, do not change the "Rates" sheet, and do not add totals or other cells. Save the document in its current format (keep the name orders.fods).

Use only LibreOffice Calc. Do not close LibreOffice.
