Share a Space with another cua.ai account by email or account ID. A **viewer**
joins with their own named cursor and watches; an **editor** can also use the
Space. They open it as `relay:<machine>` from `cua spaces ls` or the app.

```bash test="cli-shape" id="cua-spaces-share"
cua spaces share local:studio bob@example.com                 # viewer
cua spaces share local:studio bob@example.com --role editor   # change the role
cua spaces shares local:studio                                # who, and who is connected
cua spaces unshare local:studio bob@example.com               # at once
cua spaces unshare local:studio --all                         # nobody; leaves the relay
```

In the app, **Share** in a Space's toolbar opens the same list.

## What each role can do

| | Viewer | Editor |
|---|---|---|
| Presence, with their own named cursor | yes | yes |
| Watch the desktop stream | view only | yes |
| Pointer and keyboard input | refused | yes, as a human |
| Shell, files, clipboard, MCP | refused | yes |

The Space's own cua-spacesd enforces the role, not the caller. A refused call
fails with `PermissionDenied` (`view-only share: <who> cannot call <method>`).

## How it works

Sharing uses the relay and allowlist that [unattended access](/spaces/guides/unattended-access)
uses:

- A host (`relay:<id>` from `cua host setup`) is shared directly.
- Any other Space is published on the relay on its first share. The owner's
  account registers a machine for it, and the Space's own driver dials out
  over WSS, so no inbound port opens.
- The relay keeps two lists per machine: `allow` (editors) and `viewers`.
- Invitees need a verified email or account ID and an [enrolled device](/spaces/guides/unattended-access#reach-it-from-your-devices).
- Removing someone, or changing their role, cuts their open streams at once.

```rust test="excerpt" id="rs-share-space" source="libs/cua/crates/cua-spaces/tests/share_space_e2e.rs#rs-share-space"
let shares = ada
    .share_space(&info.id, "bob@example.com", ShareRole::Viewer)
    .await
    .unwrap();
```

The SDK call in other languages is `space.share(who, role)`, with
`space.unshare(who)` and `space.shares()`. The MCP tools are `share_space`,
`unshare_space` and `space_shares`.

## Confirmation

Sharing asks you first:

- Through `cua daemon` (the app, `cua mcp`, agents), with Touch ID or your
  login password. An agent can ask to share a Space; only you can allow it.
- The `cua` CLI without a daemon asks on the terminal. Pass `--yes` to confirm
  up front.

Removing someone needs no confirmation.

## Reach a Space from your other devices

To reach a Space from your phone or another computer without sharing it with
anyone, publish it on the relay:

```bash test="cli-shape" id="cua-spaces-relay-register"
cua spaces relay-register local:studio      # prints relay:<machine>
cua spaces relay-unregister local:studio
```

```rust test="excerpt" id="rs-relay-register" source="libs/cua/crates/cua-spaces/tests/share_space_e2e.rs#rs-relay-register"
let reg = ada.relay_register(&info.id).await.unwrap();
```

Only your account's enrolled devices reach it until you share it. The MCP
tools are `relay_register_space` and `relay_unregister_space`.

## Audit

- `cua spaces shares` lists who has access now.
- The owner's share log (`~/.cua/shares-audit.jsonl`, hash-chained) records
  every share, removal, attach and detach.
- The relay's audit log (`cua devices audit`) records `viewer_added`,
  `share_added`, `share_removed` and each shared access.
- The Space's access log records every relay caller, including refused
  view-only calls (`refused ProcessService (view-only share)`).

## Limits

| Space | Shareable | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Host (`relay:<id>`) | yes | Already on the relay. |
| Local container or VM | yes, while this machine runs it | Its driver dials the relay. Suspending it disconnects everyone. |
| Cloud | yes | Its driver dials the relay from the cloud. |
| Added by address (`direct:`) | yes, if its driver supports `relay_attach` | Older drivers report `capability_missing`. |
| A Space shared with you | no | Only the owner can share (`permission_denied`). |

- The relay shares with accounts, not teams: share with each member.
- Presence cursors travel on the relay stream, not QUIC datagrams, so remote
  cursors update a little less often than on your own network.
- A cloud Space that is released (for example a paused persistent agent)
  leaves the relay. Share it again after it is created again.

<Callout type="warn">
  An editor gets full control of the Space, including anything signed in
  there. Share as a viewer unless they need to act.
</Callout>
