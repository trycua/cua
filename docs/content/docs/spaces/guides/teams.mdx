Cua Spaces is bring-your-own compute: you connect your own computers, and Cua
does not rent them out. The plans cover the app, sharing and the hosted relay
that lets your devices reach your computers. Current prices are on
[cua.ai/pricing](https://cua.ai/pricing), which is the source of truth.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Live sessions | Remote hours |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 2 | 20 a month |
| Pro | $18 a month or $180 a year, one person | 5 | 200 a month |
| Teams | $30 per seat a month or $300 per seat a year, 1 to 100 seats | 5 per seat | 200 per seat a month, pooled across the team |

Every plan includes Spaces on every computer you connect and Teleport. Pro adds
more live sessions and remote hours. Teams adds sharing a Space with your team,
team-owned computers, roles and device approval. Sharing is for teams: Free and
Pro accounts without a team cannot share a Space. SSO and audit export are not
included yet.

## What counts toward the limits

**Remote hours** are the time you spend using your computers from somewhere
else. Using a computer from itself is free.

A **live session** is one person or agent holding a stream open to a computer
through the relay, such as a desktop view, a terminal or an agent's process
stream. Each hour with at least one live session counts as one remote hour, per
calendar month (UTC).

Spaces you reach directly, for example on your own network, don't use the relay
and don't count.

## See your usage

Open **Settings** in Cua Spaces. The usage row shows your live sessions and
remote hours this month, for example 1 of 2 live sessions and 6.5 of 20 remote
hours, and the plan card shows your plan. To read the same numbers in a
terminal, run `cua usage`.

When you have used 80% of your remote hours, the app shows a notice with an
**Upgrade** button. Nothing stops at the limit: your Spaces keep working.

## Upgrade to Pro

1. Press **Upgrade** or **Get Pro** in the app, or open
   [cua.ai/pricing](https://cua.ai/pricing) and choose **Get Pro**.
2. Sign in or create a Cua account.
3. Pay with Stripe Checkout.

Pro has no trial, and Free stays available. Change or cancel a plan at any time
on the [billing page](https://app.cua.ai/billing). See [Cancel](#cancel) for
refunds.

## Buy Teams

Teams turns the computers your team already has into shared desktops for people
and their agents. Computers belong to the team instead of one person's account,
an owner and admins decide who can do what, and a new device must be approved
before it can reach a team computer.

Teams starts at 1 seat, so you can begin with just yourself and add seats as
people join. Two seats give 10 live sessions and 400 remote hours a month for the
whole team.

1. Open [app.cua.ai/buy/teams](https://app.cua.ai/buy/teams) and sign in or
   create a Cua account.
2. Name your team and choose the number of seats and monthly or yearly billing.
3. Pay with Stripe Checkout. Teams is for business use: Stripe asks for your
   company name and address, and you can add your company's VAT or GST ID.

There is no trial. The person who buys is the team's owner.

## Create your team and invite people

Naming the team in checkout creates it, and you join it as its **owner**. After
payment the Team page at [app.cua.ai/team](https://app.cua.ai/team) shows
"Invite your team".

Teams are invitation only. Enter an email address and choose a role. The person
gets an email, signs in with that address and joins. Invitees do not pay and do
not see checkout.

- Members plus pending invitations cannot exceed the seats you pay for, and you
  take one seat yourself. On a 1-seat team, the Team page shows **Add seats**
  before your first invite. It opens Stripe, where you raise the quantity (see
  [Seats and billing](#seats-and-billing)).
- Until the first payment completes, a new team can invite up to 5 people.
- Revoke a pending invitation from the same page.

Cua Spaces shows your teams in **Settings, Teams**: each team with its members,
their roles and pending invitations marked "(Invited)". Press **Manage team** to
invite people and change roles in the account app, or **Billing and plans** to
change seats and billing.

## Roles

A team has one owner, and any number of admins and members.

| | Owner | Admin | Member |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open team computers (from an approved device) | yes | yes | yes |
| Request approval for a new device | not needed | yes | yes |
| Leave the team | no | yes | yes |
| Invite people | yes | yes | no |
| Remove members | yes, admins too | members only | no |
| Add and remove team computers | yes | yes | no |
| Approve or deny devices | yes | yes | no |
| Make a member an admin, or an admin a member | yes | no | no |
| Cancel the subscription, delete the team | yes | no | no |

The owner's role cannot change and the owner cannot leave: to close a team,
cancel the subscription first, then delete the team. The owner's own devices need
no approval. Admins cannot remove other admins or the owner.

Owners and admins remove a member in the Members list on the Team page. Removal
takes effect at once, and the person's device approvals for the team are deleted.
A member leaves with **Leave team** on the same page.

## Team computers

A **team computer** belongs to the team. Every member, and their agents, sees it
in the Cua Spaces app and can open it, from a device an admin has approved. A
**personal computer** stays on your own account, as described in
[Add a computer](/spaces/guides/add-a-computer), and only you and the accounts
you [share it with](/spaces/guides/share-a-space) can reach it.

| | Personal computer | Team computer |
|---|---|---|
| Belongs to | One account | The team |
| Who sees it | You, and accounts you share it with | Every member |
| Who can open it | You, and people you share it with | Every member, as an editor, from an approved device |
| Who adds or removes it | You | Owners and admins |

Team members have full use of a team computer: there are no view-only team
members. To let someone only watch, share a Space with them as a viewer instead.

### Pair a computer to the team

Pairing needs Cua Spaces 0.8.0 or later. An owner or admin does this.

1. In the Cua Spaces app, open **Machines**, press **Add a machine** and choose
   your team next to **For** at the bottom of the sheet. Then pick **Open a
   link**, **Scan a QR code**, **Run a command** or **Enter a code**. Each one
   carries a one-time pairing code that looks like `K7QM-4XPB-2RHD`. For the
   personal version of these steps, see [Add a computer](/spaces/guides/add-a-computer).
2. On the computer you are adding, open the link, or run the install command the
   app shows. With the `cua` CLI installed, the code alone is enough:

```bash test="cli-shape" id="cua-host-setup-pair-team"
cua host setup --pair K7QM-4XPB-2RHD
```

If the computer already has the Cua Spaces app, you don't need the command:
open **Add a machine** in the app there, choose **Have a code? Enter it**, and
paste the code or the link. The app asks before it joins.

The computer joins the team that made the code, with no sign-in on that computer.
A code works once and expires after 30 minutes; while the sheet is open, it
renews by itself. Revoke an unused one from the sheet's **⋯** menu.

The computer then appears under **Machines** at
[app.cua.ai/machines](https://app.cua.ai/machines), where an owner or admin can
remove it from the team. Removing a computer does not delete anything on it.

Mac, Windows and Linux computers can all be team computers, and all join with
`cua host setup`. See [Install](/spaces/guides/install) to get the `cua` CLI.
Members open team computers from the Cua Spaces app (macOS 26 or later) or the
Windows and Linux apps on the [download page](https://cua.ai/download).

### Approve a device

A member's device can open team computers only once an owner or admin approves
it.

1. The member signs in on a new device and asks for approval.
2. The request shows under **Device approvals** at
   [app.cua.ai/machines](https://app.cua.ai/machines) while it waits. The member
   sees that it is waiting for an admin.
3. An owner or admin chooses **Approve** or **Deny**. A denied device cannot open
   team computers.

Members see their own devices. Owners and admins see everyone's. Removing a
member deletes their approvals.

### Who can open a team computer

A person can open a team computer when all of these hold. The relay checks them
with the Cua account service and caches the answer for up to a minute.

- They are a member of the team. Their role is read from the team itself, so a
  removed member loses access at once.
- The computer is on the team's list.
- Their device is approved (the owner's devices always are).

### Share a Space with the team

To make a Space you already have a team computer, share it with the team. Every
member of the team, and their agents, then sees it and can open it from a device
an admin has approved, either as **Can edit** (full control) or **Can view**
(watch only).

- **In the app:** press **Share** on the Space. If you own or manage a team on
  the Teams plan, the Share window shows a **Share with** button for each of
  your teams.
- **In a terminal:** run `cua spaces share <space> team:<team id>`, or
  `cua team add-machine <team> <space>`. `cua team ls` shows the team ID.
- Only an owner or admin of the team can do this, and only for a Space they
  own. A team can hold as many shared computers as its seats allow.
- Choose **Can view** or **Can edit** for the whole team. Share again with the
  other role to change it.
- To stop, remove the team in the Share window, or run
  `cua spaces unshare <space> team:<team id>`.

You can also share a Space with one person in your team. Sharing is for teams:
you cannot share with anyone outside your team. See
[Share a Space](/spaces/guides/share-a-space).

## Seats and billing

Seats, the monthly or yearly period, the card and invoices are managed in the
Stripe billing portal: [app.cua.ai/billing](https://app.cua.ai/billing), then
**Manage billing**. Only the owner buys and changes the plan.

- **Add seats:** you pay a prorated amount for the rest of the current period,
  added to your next invoice. The maximum is 100 seats.
- **Remove seats:** you get a prorated credit on future invoices. Credits are not
  refunded in cash. You cannot go below 1 seat.
- **Switch between monthly and yearly:** in the same portal. Stripe prorates the
  change.

See the [Plan Terms](https://cua.ai/spaces-terms) for the details.

## Cancel

Cancel Pro or Teams in the billing portal at any time. The plan stays active
until the end of the period you paid for, then you move to Free (a team moves to
Free as a whole). Your Spaces and everything in them stay on your computers.

Monthly plans have no refund for partial months. On a yearly plan, cancelling
within 14 days of the first yearly charge refunds it in full, and after that
there is no refund for the unused part of the year. The
[Plan Terms](https://cua.ai/spaces-terms) have the full text.
