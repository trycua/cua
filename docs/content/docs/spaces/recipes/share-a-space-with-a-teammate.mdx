---
title: Share a Space with a teammate
description: 'Let a teammate watch or use one of your Spaces from their own Cua account.'
---

Give a teammate a view of one of your Spaces, or let them use it.

## You need

- A Space running on your Mac, and Cua Spaces signed in to your Cua account.
- Your teammate's Cua account email.
- Your teammate signed in to Cua Spaces on their own Mac.

## Steps

1. In Cua Spaces, open **Settings > Experiments** and switch on **Sharing**. A new install has it off.
2. Click your Space in the sidebar, then click **Share**.
3. Type your teammate's email in **Email or account ID**.
4. Choose **Can view** to let them watch, or **Can edit** to let them use the Space too.
5. Click **Share**, then approve with Touch ID or your Mac password.
6. Ask your teammate to open Cua Spaces. The Space is in their list.

## It worked if

- Your teammate's email shows in the Share window with the role you chose.
- A dot appears next to it while they are connected.
- Your teammate sees the Space in their list and can open it.

## If it didn't

- **There's no Share button.** Switch on **Sharing** in **Settings > Experiments**.
- **The Share window says "Sign in to cua.ai to share."** Sign in to Cua Spaces, then try again.
- **Your teammate can't see the Space.** They need to sign in to Cua Spaces with the email you used. If their Mac needs enrolling, they open **Settings > Devices**, click **Enroll** and choose **Sign in again**.

**Can edit** gives full control of the Space, including anything signed in
there. Choose **Can view** unless your teammate needs to act. To stop sharing,
click **Remove** next to their name.
