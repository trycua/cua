Sharing is for teams. You can share a Space with the people in your own team,
one at a time or the whole team. A **viewer** ("Can view") joins with their
own named cursor and watches. An **editor** ("Can edit") can also use the
Space. They open it from their own app or terminal as `relay:<machine>`.

<Callout title="Sharing needs a team">
  On Free and Pro without a team, the **Share** button explains this and links
  to [Teams](/spaces/guides/teams), which starts at 1 seat. Start a team, invite
  the people you want to share with, then share.
</Callout>

To share from the app, press **Share** in the Space's toolbar, enter the email
address or account ID of someone in your team, and choose **Can view** or
**Can edit**. The same list shows
who has access and who is connected. Cua Spaces asks you to confirm first:
Touch ID or your login password on a Mac, Windows Hello or your account password
on Windows, and your password through polkit on Linux.

<Callout title="Sharing on Linux">
  Sharing on Linux needs Cua Spaces installed from the **.deb**, and a desktop
  session with a polkit authentication agent running (XFCE, GNOME and KDE start
  one). The .deb installs the password prompt; the AppImage cannot, so it cannot
  share. A remote desktop (xrdp, VNC) works too: you get the same password
  prompt. If sharing fails at once with no prompt, update to the latest .deb.
  See [Linux packages](/spaces/guides/install#linux-packages).
</Callout>

You can share with one teammate at a time, or with the whole team at once. See
[People and teams](#people-and-teams).

From a terminal:

```bash test="cli-shape" id="cua-spaces-share"
cua spaces share local:studio bob@example.com                 # viewer
cua spaces share local:studio bob@example.com --role editor   # change the role
cua spaces share local:studio team:acme --role viewer         # the whole team, to watch
cua spaces shares local:studio                                # who, and who is connected
cua spaces unshare local:studio bob@example.com               # at once
cua spaces unshare local:studio --all                         # nobody; leaves the relay
```

## People and teams

- **One person.** Type their email or account ID. They must be a member of
  your team: if they are not, you see "<who> isn't in your team. Invite them
  to your team first." You choose **Can view** or **Can edit** for each person.
- **A whole team.** Every member of the team, and their agents, can open the
  Space, as **Can view** (watch only) or **Can edit**. Only an owner or admin
  of a team on the [Teams plan](/spaces/guides/teams) can do this, and only for
  a Space they own. See
  [Share a Space with the team](/spaces/guides/teams#share-a-space-with-the-team).
- **No team.** Free and Pro accounts without a team cannot share. The
  command fails with "Sharing is for teams. Start a team to share this Space."

<Callout title="If you shared with people outside your team before">
  Those shares stopped when sharing became team-only. The Space owner sees one
  line in the app listing who was removed, with an **Invite to your team**
  button. Once they join your team, share again.
</Callout>

## What each role can do

| | Viewer | Editor |
|---|---|---|
| Presence, with their own named cursor | yes | yes |
| Watch the desktop stream | view only | yes |
| Pointer and keyboard input | refused | yes, as a human |
| Shell, files, clipboard, MCP | refused | yes |

The Space's own cua-spacesd enforces the role, not the caller. A refused call
fails with `PermissionDenied` (`view-only share: <who> cannot call <method>`).

A whole-team share has the same two roles, **Can view** and **Can edit**. To
change it, share with the team again with the other role.

## How it works

Sharing uses the same relay and allowlist as an
[added computer](/spaces/guides/add-a-computer#add-a-computer-from-a-terminal):

- A host (`relay:<id>` from `cua host setup`) is shared directly.
- Any other Space is published on the relay on its first share. The owner's
  account registers a computer for it, and the Space's own driver dials out
  over WSS, so no inbound port opens.
- The relay keeps two lists per computer: `allow` (editors) and `viewers`.
- Invitees need a verified email or account ID and an [enrolled device](/spaces/guides/add-a-computer#reach-it-from-your-other-devices).
- Removing someone, or changing their role, cuts their open streams at once.
- Team membership comes from your team in your Cua account, not from the
  relay. The Cua account service checks it when you share and again each time
  someone opens the Space, and the relay enforces the answer. If someone
  leaves your team, they lose access to what you shared with them. If a team
  is deleted, its shares stop.
- A team share works differently. The Space goes on the team's list of
  computers, and each time someone opens it, the relay checks with the Cua
  account service that they are still in the team and that their device is
  approved.

```rust test="excerpt" id="rs-share-space" source="libs/cua/crates/cua-spaces/tests/share_space_e2e.rs#rs-share-space"
let shares = ada
    .share_space(&info.id, "bob@example.com", ShareRole::Viewer)
    .await
    .unwrap();
```

The SDK call in other languages is `space.share(who, role)`, with
`space.unshare(who)` and `space.shares()`. The MCP tools are `share_space`,
`unshare_space` and `space_shares`.

## Confirmation

Sharing asks you first:

- Through `cua daemon` (the app, `cua mcp`, agents), with Touch ID or your
  login password. An agent can ask to share a Space; only you can allow it.
- The `cua` CLI without a daemon asks on the terminal. Pass `--yes` to confirm
  up front.

Removing someone needs no confirmation.

## Reach a Space from your other devices

To reach a Space from your phone or another computer without sharing it with
anyone, publish it on the relay:

```bash test="cli-shape" id="cua-spaces-relay-register"
cua spaces relay-register local:studio      # prints relay:<machine>
cua spaces relay-unregister local:studio
```

```rust test="excerpt" id="rs-relay-register" source="libs/cua/crates/cua-spaces/tests/share_space_e2e.rs#rs-relay-register"
let reg = ada.relay_register(&info.id).await.unwrap();
```

Only your account's enrolled devices reach it until you share it. The MCP
tools are `relay_register_space` and `relay_unregister_space`.

## Audit

- `cua spaces shares` lists who has access now.
- The owner's share log (`~/.cua/shares-audit.jsonl`, hash-chained) records
  every share, removal, attach and detach.
- The relay's audit log (`cua devices audit`) records `viewer_added`,
  `share_added`, `share_removed` and each shared access.
- The Space's access log records every relay caller, including refused
  view-only calls (`refused ProcessService (view-only share)`).

## Limits

| Space | Shareable | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Host (`relay:<id>`) | yes | Already on the relay. |
| Local container or VM | yes, while this computer runs it | Its driver dials the relay. Suspending it disconnects everyone. |
| In your own cloud (AWS, Google Cloud, Modal) | yes | Its driver dials the relay from the cloud. |
| Added by address (`direct:`) | yes, if its driver supports `relay_attach` | Older drivers report `capability_missing`. |
| A Space shared with you | no | Only the owner can share (`permission_denied`). |

- You can share with a person in your team or with the whole team, as
  **Can view** or **Can edit**.
- Presence cursors travel on the relay stream, not QUIC datagrams, so remote
  cursors update a little less often than on your own network.
- A Space that is released (for example a paused persistent agent in your
  cloud) leaves the relay. Share it again after it is created again.

<Callout type="warn">
  An editor gets full control of the Space, including anything signed in
  there. Share as a viewer unless they need to act.
</Callout>
