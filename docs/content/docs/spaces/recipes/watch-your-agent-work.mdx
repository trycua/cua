See what your agent is doing in a Space, as it does it.

## You need

- Cua Spaces, and a Space that is running. In the app, **New Space** makes one.
- A coding agent connected to Cua (Claude Code, Codex or another). The **AI agents** page in the app's first-run setup does this.
- A task for the agent, such as "open Firefox in my Space and look up today's weather".

## Steps

1. Open Cua Spaces and click your Space in the sidebar.
2. Click **Open**. The Space's desktop opens in its own window.
3. In your agent, ask it to work in that Space and show it to you. For example: "In my Space called `<name>`, open Firefox and look up today's weather. Show me the Space while you work."
4. Watch the window. The agent's cursor moves, clicks and types on the Space's desktop.
5. To keep watching while you do something else, click **Picture in picture** on the Space. The desktop floats above your other windows.

## It worked if

- You see the Space's desktop change as the agent works.
- The agent's cursor moves on its own, and your own pointer is separate from it.

## If it didn't

- **The agent says it can't find the Space.** Give it the Space's exact name from the sidebar. If the agent doesn't know Cua, open **Settings** and run the **AI agents** setup again, then restart the agent.
- **The window says "Not reachable" or stays blank.** The Space may be off. Click **Turn on** (or **Resume**) on the Space, wait for it to start, and open it again.
- **Nothing opens when the agent says it did.** Open the Space yourself (steps 1 and 2).
